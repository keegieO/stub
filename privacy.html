Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version: Stub keeps everything on your own computer. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. It never sends your order information anywhere, and it never sells or shares it.

This policy explains how the Stub browser extension ("Stub", "we", "us") handles information. Stub is published by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME].

What Stub stores

To remind you about return deadlines, Stub saves the following, only in your browser:

  • For each tracked item: the item name, store name, price (when shown), order date, return-by date, order number and links to the order and product pages.
  • Items you add by hand, with whatever details you type in.
  • Your choices: whether you marked an item Returned or Keeping, and your reminder settings.
  • A record of which reminders have already been shown, so you don't get the same one twice.

Where it comes from

Stub reads your Amazon order history pages (amazon.com) while you visit them in your browser and are signed in. It only looks at those pages; it does not read any other website, your email, your browsing history, or your passwords or payment details. It does not run on checkout or payment pages.

Where it is kept

All of the information above is stored using your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. Stub does not operate any servers and makes no network requests of its own. The information is not synced to your Google account or to other devices.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, cookies or advertising of any kind.
  • We do not use your information for credit decisions, lending or any purpose unrelated to reminding you about returns.
  • We do not insert affiliate links or change the pages you visit, other than showing a small, temporary "return windows saved" notice on the order history page.

Stub's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions Stub asks for

  • Read your Amazon order history pages: to find each order's return date.
  • Storage: to save your items and settings on your device.
  • Alarms: to check twice an hour whether a reminder is due.
  • Notifications: to show you desktop reminders.

Your control

You can see everything Stub has saved in its ledger page. You can export it to a CSV file or delete all of it at any time from Settings → Your data. Uninstalling Stub removes all of its stored information from your browser.

Children

Stub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly receive information from children. Because Stub does not send any information to us, we hold no information about any user.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of Stub ever offers an optional feature that sends information off your device (for example, phone reminders), it will be off by default, clearly explained, and only turned on if you choose it.

Never miss a return window again.

Stub is a free Chrome extension that saves the return date for your Amazon orders and reminds you before it closes. Bought something somewhere else? Add it by hand.

COMING SOON TO THE CHROME WEB STORE

No account · No servers · Nothing leaves your browser

How it works

  1. Install Stub and open your Amazon order history once while signed in.
  2. Stub reads the return-by date on each order and saves it in your browser.
  3. You get a reminder a few days before a window closes, and again on the last day.

Private by design

Everything stays on your computer. No tracking, no analytics, no data sold, ever. Read the privacy policy.
